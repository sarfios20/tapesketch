Tapesketch

Three things and you are done

  1. Click, move, click. That is a wall. Esc ends it.
  2. Type 350 and Enter while you draw and the segment measures exactly that.
  3. EXPORT → PDF to scale. It prints and it measures with a ruler.

Tapesketch needs a mouse and a keyboard. You draw by dragging, you type the sizes in centimeters and almost everything has a one-letter shortcut. On a touch screen there is no way to use it, so I tell you instead of showing you a dead canvas. Open it on a computer.

Keyboard shortcuts

Walls

click starts and fixes each segment · 350Enter makes it exactly 350 cm · Esc ends

d puts a door and w a window on the wall you are pointing at, without selecting anything first. On top of a door, d flips the way it opens and then takes it out. 90d makes it 90 cm.

With a wall segment selected, Ctrl+↑ and Ctrl+↓ change its thickness by 1 cm

B locks or unlocks the walls

Shapes and furniture

R switches to shapes and cycles rectangle, circle and L-shape · drag to create one

With one item selected, type 120x60 or bed and Enter. On an L, the inner point changes the width of its arms.

Moving things

The top handle rotates · Ctrl+G turns 90° · PgUp and PgDn send it front or back

arrows nudge 0.5 cm; with Shift, 10 cm

drag moves and sticks to walls and corners · Shift without snapping

Selecting

In select mode, drag picks by box · Ctrl+click adds · Ctrl+A all

Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy and paste · Del deletes · Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo

Copies of the room

+ COPY duplicates the copy you are on · click another copy to work on it · its name renames, duplicates or deletes it

P places a point, such as an outlet: type its label and Enter. Points are in every copy.

With pieces selected, Same in every copy by the measurements puts them in every copy, dashed

Getting around

wheel zooms · middle button pans · Home fits everything

Export to PDF

Name and company go in the title block. They are saved in this browser, not inside the plan: they do not travel when you export it.
